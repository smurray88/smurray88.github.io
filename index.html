About

I started as an application developer at UPS in 2010, right out of the University of Delaware's computer engineering program. Over the next twelve years I worked my way up to lead developer, building native Android, Xamarin and web apps and serving as Scrum Master along the way.

In 2022 I moved into management and led the complete rebuild of UPS's customer-facing mobile app and its RESTful services. The new app started 50% faster and doubled its NPS. I also hired more than 50 engineers, coordinated vendor and internal teams on a major rewards launch, and brought GitHub Copilot into our day-to-day work.

In October 2026 I joined Sinclair as a Senior Software Engineering Manager. What I care about hasn't changed: clear direction, healthy teams, and software that holds up in the real world.
